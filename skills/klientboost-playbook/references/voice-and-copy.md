# Voice and copy

Use this when writing headlines, service-page copy, ads, FAQs, or proof blocks.

## Voice

**Competent friend who swears a little.** First-person plural. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers over claims. Analogies that teach (sliced bread, fishing leftovers, Halloween candy). Internal jokes are fine if they humanize the team without stalling the sale.

Tone sliders:

| Turn up | Turn down |
| --- | --- |
| Specificity, pace, proof | Jargon, process-speak, "synergy" |
| Contrast with bad agencies | Insulting the buyer |
| Named tactics | Generic "best practices" |
| Accountability | Vague "we'll optimize" |
| Fun that still sells | Bit-for-bit comedy bits |

Profanity is optional and rare. They imply it ("give a s***") more than they dump it. Match the client's brand; the pattern is candor, not shock.

## Headline system

**H1 = the outcome, with a sharper adjective than the category uses.**

Patterns that recur:

- `The [category] that [business result]`
- `Get a [specific lift] in [timebox]`
- `We'll make you [excess] more [money/pipeline] than [alternative]`
- `Get [n] [options] for your goals`

**H2 = the contrast, the insult to the category, or the proof.** It should not repeat the H1.

Examples of the *shape* (do not copy wording):

- Outcome + accountability: agency that hits bigger goals / aggressive accountability
- Proof volume: more success stories than other agencies have clients
- Category jab: does your agency actually hit goals, consistently? didn't think so
- Lead magnet: the $10,000 plan we're giving away

**Section titles are opinions**, not labels. Prefer "Never bring an opinion to a data fight" over "Our data-driven approach."

**Subheads do the explaining.** The H2 can be cocky; the paragraph underneath has to be operational.

## CTA language

One primary action. Name the artifact, not the meeting.

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| Contact us | Get your free marketing plan |
| Book a call | See the 3 pricing options for your goals |
| Learn more | Take our ideas, even if we don't work together |
| Submit | Continue my marketing plan |

Repeat the same CTA. Do not invent a new verb on each section.

## Proof language

Stack proof in this order:

1. **Live operating metric** (goal-hit rate this quarter)
2. **Scale metric** (clients, spend under management, case-study count)
3. **Timeboxed result** (average ROI lift in 90 days; payback in 3–6 months)
4. **Named buyers** (role + company, not "a leading SaaS brand")
5. **Operator celebrities** in the same craft (people the CMO already reads)
6. **Objection-mapped reviews** (cost, speed, chemistry, "we mess up too")
7. **Culture as competence** (best-place-to-work, specialists per channel)

Rules:

- Every number needs a denominator and a window (`63% ROI increase in the first three months`, `83% of client goals in Q2`).
- Show negative reviews on purpose. Accountability copy only works if you admit misses.
- "Unlimited references" is stronger than another logo strip.

## FAQ as sales

Title the FAQ like a dare ("Go ahead, stump us"). Answer with a decision rule, not a brochure.

Cover: spend needed, fee, time-to-launch, time-to-results, contract length, pay-for-performance, forecasting, reporting, output volume. Reverse-engineer numbers from the goal (`20 conversions/day × $10 CPA = $200/day`).

If the honest answer is "it depends," say what it depends on and point to the plan.

## Micro-copy habits

- Parenthetical asides that land a second punch
- "We don't usually brag, but…" then brag with evidence
- Lists of synonyms for "free" when killing a cost objection
- Named doodle callouts that rank differentiators (1st, 2nd, 3rd most important)
- Checklists that make the work visible (40-point "do you even do this" lists)

## Rewrite patterns

**Generic agency**

> We are a full-service digital marketing agency dedicated to helping brands grow through data-driven paid media and conversion optimization.

**Playbook rewrite**

> We're the team that treats your ad account like a revenue engine, not a lead slot machine. Ads, landing pages, and CRO sit in one engagement. If a channel misses the goal, we pull it.

**Generic CTA**

> Ready to take your marketing to the next level? Get in touch today.

**Playbook rewrite**

> Get the plan we'd run: competitors, keyword gaps, mocked ads and pages, and three ways to pay for it. Takes hours on our side. Costs you nothing.

**Generic landing-page subhead**

> Our designers create beautiful, high-converting landing pages tailored to your brand.

**Playbook rewrite**

> One page. One action. Copy that matches the ad they clicked. We test the form, the hero, and the ask until CPA moves — not until the mockup looks pretty.

## What not to write

- "Welcome to our agency"
- "Passionate team of experts"
- "Holistic, 360-degree solutions"
- Unsourced "up to 10x" claims
- Dual CTAs ("Book a demo or download the whitepaper") on the same hero
- Process theater ("discover, define, deliver") with no goal math
