# Pattern evidence

Use the narrowest evidence label that the source supports:

| Level | Meaning | Safe claim |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | A real product currently uses the pattern | The pattern exists in this context |
| Common | Several relevant products use it | The pattern is established, not necessarily effective |
| Reported | A credible source reports an outcome | The reported result held in the source context |
| Tested | A controlled test or strong causal design supports it | The mechanism likely caused the measured change |

Assess transfer risk across audience, lifecycle, device, business model, trust stakes, regulation, and brand. Treat large differences as reasons to test, not reasons to copy.

When researching examples:

1. Search by user goal and lifecycle moment.
2. Compare at least three relevant flows when available.
3. Record the entry condition, sequence, requested effort, value exchange, and recovery path.
4. Separate the reusable mechanism from visual treatment.
5. Cite the source and observation date.
6. State what remains unknown.
